Frequently asked questions

The camera doesn't open.

Allow camera access in Settings › Doc Scanner › Camera, then try scanning again.

Text recognition (OCR) missed some words.

Scan in good light, hold the phone flat over the page and make sure the text is in focus. Recognition happens on your iPhone and works best on printed text.

Where are my scans saved?

Inside the app on your iPhone. You can export or share any document as a PDF.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Scans, PDFs and recognised text stay on your iPhone.

How do I remove ads?

In Settings you can watch a short rewarded ad to hide ads for 24 hours.
