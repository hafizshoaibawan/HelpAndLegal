Privacy Policy — Kidz Uploader

Effective 28 November 2026 · OamiSoft Private Ltd

In short: Kidz Uploader is a private upload tool for one YouTube channel, Kidz Playing Room. It is used only by that channel's owner. It does not collect data from anyone else and does not sell or share data.

What the tool does

Kidz Uploader uses the YouTube Data API to upload the channel owner's own videos to the Kidz Playing Room channel and to set their title, description, tags, thumbnail, subtitles, audience setting ("made for kids") and playlists. It can also read the channel's own video and playlist list, change a video's privacy setting, and delete a test upload.

Google account data

  • The channel owner signs in with Google and allows the YouTube permissions listed above. Google returns an access token that lets the tool act on that channel only.
  • The token is stored privately in the owner's own workspace and is used only to manage the owner's channel. It is never shared, sold or used for advertising.
  • The tool does not read, collect or store data about viewers, subscribers or any other YouTube users.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API use also follows the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

The Kidz Playing Room channel makes videos for children, but this tool is used only by the adult channel owner. It does not collect any information from children.

Removing access

The channel owner can remove the tool's access at any time at Google Account › Third-party connections. Removing access makes the stored token stop working.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top.
