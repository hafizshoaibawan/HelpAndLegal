Privacy Policy — Locked Gallery

Effective 24 November 2026 · OamiSoft (Hafiz M Shoaib Akram)

In short: Your private photos and videos stay on your iPhone. We never upload, see or receive them. Ads in the app are provided by Google.

We (OamiSoft) don't have accounts, don't run our own servers for this app, and don't sell your data.

This policy explains what information the iOS app Locked Gallery ("the app") handles, who receives it and the choices you have.

Your photos, videos and passcode

  • Stored only on your device. Photos and videos you add are copied into the app's own private storage on your iPhone. They are not uploaded to us or to any server, and we cannot see them.
  • Photo library access. The app asks for access to your photo library only so you can choose items to import and, if you ask, save items back to Photos. After importing, the app can remove the originals from your Photos library, and iOS always asks you to confirm before anything is deleted.
  • Passcode. Your passcode (and any decoy passcode) is stored in the iOS Keychain on this device only. We never receive it and cannot recover it for you. If you forget it, the hidden items cannot be unlocked.
  • Recently Deleted. Items you delete in the app are kept in Recently Deleted for 30 days so you can restore them, then removed permanently.
  • Device backups. Items in the app are included in your device's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. That backup is controlled by you and Apple, not by us.

Advertising and analytics by Google

The app is free and shows ads. To do this it uses services from Google, which processes data under its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob (ads) and Google User Messaging Platform (consent). To show and measure ads, and to limit fraud, Google may collect: your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking — see below), other device identifiers, IP address and the approximate location derived from it, device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version), and how you interact with ads.
  • Google Firebase (app measurement and stability). Firebase may collect app usage events (for example, which screens are opened), crash and performance diagnostics, a Firebase installation ID, and device information. This helps us understand how the app is used and fix problems.

This data may be used to show you personalised ads and may be linked to identifiers used across other companies' apps and websites (tracking), but only when you allow it. If you don't allow tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalised with your advertising identifier.

More information: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Your choices

  • Tracking. The first time the app needs it, iOS asks whether the app may track you. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland). Where the law requires it, the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load, where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising.
  • Personalised ads. You can also reset or limit your advertising identifier in Settings › Privacy & Security › Apple Advertising.
  • Removing ads for a while. The app may offer to watch a rewarded ad to hide ads for 24 hours. This is optional.
  • Deleting data. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on your device. To ask about data held by Google for advertising, use the Google links above; for anything else, email us.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data retention and security

We do not run our own servers for this app and do not receive or store your personal data. Data kept on your device stays until you delete it in the app or delete the app. Data collected by Google is kept according to Google's policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's update notes.
