Frequently asked questions

I forgot my passcode. Can you unlock the app?

No. Your passcode is stored only in the iOS Keychain on your iPhone and is never sent to us, so we can't see or reset it. If you can't remember it, you can tap Reset on the lock screen. This permanently deletes every folder, photo and video in the app and erases both passcodes, so please keep your passcode safe.

What is the decoy passcode?

You can set a second passcode that opens a separate, harmless set of folders. If someone makes you open the app, use the decoy passcode and they will only see the decoy folders.

I deleted a photo by mistake.

Deleted items stay in Recently Deleted inside the app for 30 days. Open it and restore the item. After 30 days items are removed permanently.

Will my hidden photos be backed up?

Items in the app are included in your iPhone's own backup (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have backups turned on. Nothing is uploaded to us.

Can I remove the original photos from my Photos library?

Yes. After importing, the app can delete the originals from Photos. iOS always asks you to confirm first, and deleted originals go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album.

How do I remove ads?

In Settings you can watch a short rewarded ad to hide ads for 24 hours.
