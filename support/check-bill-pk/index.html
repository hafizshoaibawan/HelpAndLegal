Frequently asked questions

Which companies are supported?

Electricity: LESCO, IESCO, FESCO, GEPCO, MEPCO, PESCO, HESCO, SEPCO, QESCO, TESCO and HAZECO. Gas: SNGPL and SSGC.

It says no bill was found.

Check the 14-digit reference number (or consumer number for gas) on a printed bill and that you chose the right company. Bills for the new month appear only after the company publishes them, and the company's bill website may be down for a while. Try again later.

Do you see my reference numbers or bills?

No. The app sends your reference number straight from your phone to the public bill website, and saved bills stay on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.

Can I save a bill as an image or PDF?

Yes. Open the bill and use the share or save button. Saving an image to Photos asks for permission to add to your photo library.

How do I remove ads?

In Settings you can watch a short rewarded ad to hide ads for 24 hours.
