Frequently asked questions

Why are my results different from another speed test?

Speed depends on the test server, time of day, Wi-Fi signal and other devices on your network. The app uses Cloudflare and Apple test servers. Run a few tests and compare averages.

Why does the app ask for my location?

iOS only lets apps read the name of your Wi-Fi network if location access is allowed. The app uses it for that alone and never stores or sends your location. You can refuse and still run tests.

The test fails or stops.

Make sure you're connected to the internet, turn off VPNs or content blockers that may block test servers, then try again.

How do I delete my test history?

Open History in the app, choose a test and delete it. History is stored only on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes it all.

How do I remove ads?

In Settings you can watch a short rewarded ad to hide ads for 24 hours.
