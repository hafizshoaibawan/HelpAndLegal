Frequently asked questions

The app says it can't reach my target size.

Very small targets can't always be met for large photos. Choose a larger size in KB or a smaller pixel size (or a preset), then try again.

Is my original photo changed?

No. The app saves a new, smaller copy to your Photos library and leaves the original untouched.

Why does it ask for photo library permission?

Only to save the compressed copy. You pick photos with the iOS picker, so the app only sees the photos you choose.

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Resizing and compression happen on your iPhone.

How do I remove ads?

In Settings you can watch a short rewarded ad to hide ads for 24 hours.
